Mavo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

In short

  • Your diary, photos, weight and plan are stored only on your phone. Mavo has no user accounts.
  • Only the photo or text you choose to analyse is sent, to estimate calories with AI. We do not store your photos on our server.
  • No advertising, no selling of data and no cross-app tracking.
  • Crash reports without personal data, to improve the app.
  • You can export and delete everything from the app and exercise your rights by writing to mavo.soporte@gmail.com.

Mavo is an app that lets you log what you eat with a photo and estimates calories and macronutrients. This policy explains what data is processed, why, who receives it and what rights you have.

1. Who is responsible

Marc Dominguez Joyanes, 08225 Terrassa (Barcelona), Spain. Contact: mavo.soporte@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your phone

Your food diary, photos, weight, steps, calorie plan, profile (sex, age, height, activity, goal) and settings are stored only on your device. Mavo has no user accounts and keeps no copy of this content on its servers.

3. What leaves your phone and why

Data Purpose Recipient
The photo or text you choose to analyze Estimate foods, calories and macros Our server and Anthropic (AI provider, Claude). We do not use your photos to train models and do not store them on our server
A food name or barcode Look up nutrition values Public databases: USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts. Nothing that identifies you is sent
A random installation identifier Control free and daily analysis limits and prevent abuse Our server
IP address Rate limiting and service protection. Not stored permanently Our server
Subscription data (product purchased, status) Manage your subscription and unlock Premium features RevenueCat and the app store (Apple or Google)
Crash reports (device model, OS and app version, technical error trace) Detect and fix app crashes. They do not include your meals, photos, weight or personal data Sentry (EU servers)

There is no advertising, we do not sell data and we do not track you across apps or websites.

4. Health data and consent

Information about food, weight and goals may be considered health-related. Before your first photo or text is sent to the AI we ask for your explicit consent (GDPR art. 9(2)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time by no longer using AI analysis; the rest of the app (manual food search, barcode, diary) works without that transfer.

5. International transfers

Anthropic, RevenueCat and Sentry may process data outside the European Economic Area, in particular in the United States. These transfers rely on the safeguards provided by the GDPR, such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. Retention

  • Your diary stays on your phone until you delete it.
  • The installation identifier and quota counters are kept while the app remains in use.
  • We do not store your analyzed photos or texts on our server; the AI provider applies its own retention periods, described in its policy.

7. Your rights

You may exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability by writing to mavo.soporte@gmail.com. Inside the app you can export your data (CSV) and delete all your data from Profile. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

8. Minimum age

Mavo is intended for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly process data of younger users.

9. Security

Communication with our server is encrypted and we limit the data sent to what is strictly necessary.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will publish the new version on this page with its date.

Nutrition data sources

Nutrition values come from USDA FoodData Central (public domain), Open Food Facts (ODbL licence) and the Ciqual 2025 food composition table from Anses, the French Agency for Food, Environmental and Occupational Health & Safety (https://ciqual.anses.fr), published under the Licence Ouverte / Etalab 2.0. Some dishes use approximate average values and are labelled as such in the app.
