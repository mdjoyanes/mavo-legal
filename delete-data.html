Delete my data

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Mavo has no user accounts, so almost all of your data is only on your phone and you control it.

Inside the app

Go to Profile → Data and privacy. There you have:

  • Export my data (CSV): save a copy before deleting.
  • Delete history: removes your logged meals.
  • Delete everything, including my profile: removes the diary, photos, weight, your plan and your settings from the device.

You can also stop using AI analysis at any time (withdraw your consent on that same screen). Uninstalling the app also removes its local data from your phone.

Data outside your phone

Mavo does not store your photos or analysed texts on its server. The only thing it keeps is a random installation identifier and usage counters to control the analysis quota. If you want us to delete them, write to mavo.soporte@gmail.com and say you want your data deleted; to locate them we may ask you for that identifier.

Other providers process their own data under their policies: Apple or Google (your purchases and subscription), RevenueCat (subscription status), Sentry (crash reports without personal data) and Anthropic (AI provider). Details in the privacy policy.
