Mavo Support

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Need help with Mavo? Here are the most common answers. If you can't find yours, write to mavo.soporte@gmail.com and we'll help.

Contact

Email: mavo.soporte@gmail.com. Please include your phone model, the app version and, if you can, a screenshot of what is happening.

How accurate are the calories?

They are estimates made with artificial intelligence and public databases, and may contain errors. On the result screen you can correct the ingredients and portions; Mavo learns from your corrections. For any medical or dietary decision, talk to a professional.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Mavo cannot cancel it for you: your store manages it. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Mavo → Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Mavo → Cancel.

How do I restore my purchases?

If you were already Premium and have changed phones or reinstalled the app, use the Restore purchases button in the app, with the same Apple or Google account you paid with.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple and Google. On iPhone, at reportaproblem.apple.com; on Android, from your Google Play order history.

How do I export or delete my data?

In the app: Profile → Data and privacy. There you can export your data as CSV and delete your history or everything, including your profile. More details on the Delete my data page.

I changed phones, what happens to my diary?

Your data lives only on your phone and Mavo has no cloud copy. Before switching phones or uninstalling the app, export a copy from Profile → Data and privacy.

Why do my free analyses run out?

Mavo includes a limited number of free AI analyses, and there is also a daily limit to prevent abuse. Manual food search and barcode scanning do not use AI analyses.
