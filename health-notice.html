Health notice

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Mavo is a meal-logging tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical, nutritional or therapeutic advice.

What this means in practice

  • Calories and macronutrients are approximate estimates made with artificial intelligence and public databases, and may contain errors.
  • Mavo does not diagnose, treat or prevent any disease and does not replace a doctor or dietitian.
  • Talk to a professional before changing your diet, especially if you are pregnant, have a medical condition, take medication or have had an eating disorder.
  • If you feel your relationship with food or your body is hurting you, seek professional help. In an emergency, call your country's emergency number (112 in the EU).
